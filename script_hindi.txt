SLIDE 2 - PROMPT KYA HAI?
----------------------------
Prompt matlab — vo text jo aap AI ko
likhte ho. Jaise aap Google mein search
karte ho, waise hi AI ko prompt dete ho.
Lekin agar prompt lamba aur confusing
hoga, toh AI ka jawab bhi acha nahi hoga.
Isliye hamesha chota aur clear prompt likho!

----------------------------
SLIDE 3 - TOKEN KYA HAI?
----------------------------
Token matlab text ka chota tukda.
Jaise Main ek token hai.
Artificial Intelligence do token hai.
Jitne zyada token use hoge,
utna zyada paisa lagega.
Isliye tokens bachana matlab paisa bachana!

----------------------------
SLIDE 4 - 6 TIPS
----------------------------
Pehla tip — Hamesha chota likho.
Doosra tip — AI ko Hello mat kaho.
Teesra tip — Output ki limit lagao.
Chautha tip — Poore sentence ki jagah
keywords use karo.
Paanchwa tip — Same cheez baar baar
mat paste karo.
Chhaata tip — Saare sawaal ek saath pucho!
In 6 tips se aap bahut saare tokens
baacha sakte ho!

----------------------------
SLIDE 5 - PERFECT PROMPT FORMULA
----------------------------
Perfect prompt ke 4 parts hote hain.
Pehla — Role. AI ko batao ki vo kaun hai.
Doosra — Task. Kya karna hai.
Teesra — Format. Jawab kaisi tarah chahiye.
Chautha — Limit. Kitna chota jawab chahiye.
Example: Tu ek finance expert hai.
SIP ko 5 points mein simple Hindi mein samjhao.
Bas itna! Perfect prompt ready!
