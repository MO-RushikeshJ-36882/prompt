# Speaker Notes - Prompt Engineering Seminar

## Slide 1: Title Slide
"Good morning everyone. Today I will be presenting on a very important and trending topic — Prompt Engineering in Software Development. This topic is crucial because AI tools like ChatGPT and GitHub Copilot are changing how we write code."

## Slide 2: Agenda
"Here is today's agenda. We will start from the basics and move towards advanced techniques and real-world applications."

## Slide 3: What is Prompt Engineering?
"Prompt engineering is simply the skill of writing better instructions for AI. Just like how we give clear requirements to a junior developer, we need to give clear prompts to AI models. The better your prompt, the better the output."

## Slide 4: Why is it Important?
"Why should we care? Because AI is not going anywhere. Companies like TCS, Infosys, and startups are already using AI tools daily. If you know how to prompt well, you become 10x more productive."

## Slide 5: Applications in Software Development
"Prompt engineering is used everywhere in software development — from writing code to finding bugs, generating tests, and even writing documentation. Let me explain each one briefly."

## Slide 6: Types of Prompting
"There are different types of prompting. Zero-shot means you just give an instruction. Few-shot means you give examples. Chain-of-thought means you ask AI to think step by step. Let me show each one in detail."

## Slide 7: Technique 1 — Zero-Shot Prompting
"Zero-shot is the simplest form. You just tell the AI what to do without any example. This works well for common tasks that AI already knows."

## Slide 8: Technique 2 — Few-Shot Prompting
"In few-shot prompting, you give 2 to 5 examples so the AI understands the pattern. Then it can apply the same pattern to new inputs. Very useful for formatting and conversion tasks."

## Slide 9: Technique 3 — Chain-of-Thought
"Chain-of-thought is very powerful. Just by adding 'think step by step' to your prompt, the AI gives much more accurate answers for complex problems."

## Slide 10: Technique 4 — Role-Based Prompting
"When you give AI a role, it responds like an expert in that field. This is one of the most effective techniques. Always mention the role at the beginning of your prompt."

## Slide 11: Technique 5 — Prompt Chaining
"For big tasks, never give everything in one prompt. Break it into steps. This is called prompt chaining. Each output feeds into the next prompt. Very useful for building full applications."

## Slide 12: Best Practices
"These are the golden rules. Always be specific, mention the format, give context, and iterate. No one writes a perfect prompt on the first try — keep refining."
