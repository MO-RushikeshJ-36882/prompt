Prompt Kya Hota Hai?

❌ Galat Prompt

"Hello AI aap kaise ho, kya aap please mujhe is text ko summarize karne mein help kar sakte ho?"

✅ Sahi Prompt

"Summarize: [text]"

Token Kya Hota Hai?

"Main" = 1 Token
"Main AI use karta hun" = 5 Tokens
"Artificial Intelligence" = 2 Tokens

💰 1000 Tokens = ₹0.10 se ₹0.25
Zyada Token = Zyada Paisa! ❌
Kam Token = Kam Paisa! ✅
🎙️ "Token matlab text ka chota tukda. Jitne zyada tokens use hoge, utna zyada bill aayega. Isliye tokens bachana zaroori hai!"

6 Token Bachane ke Tips 💡

💡 Tip 1 — Chota Likho

❌ Lamba sentence
✅ Short keywords

💡 Tip 2 — No Greeting

❌ "Hello AI kaise ho"
✅ Seedha kaam ki baat

💡 Tip 3 — Output Limit

✅ "Sirf 2 lines mein batao"
✅ "3 points mein"

💡 Tip 4 — Keywords

❌ Poora sentence
✅ Bas keywords likho

💡 Tip 5 — No Repeat

❌ Baar baar paste karo
✅ "Upar diya data dekho"

💡 Tip 6 — Batch Karo

❌ 5 alag messages
✅ Sab ek saath pucho

Perfect Prompt Formula

ROLE → AI ko batao ki vo kaun hai
TASK → Kya karna hai
FORMAT → Jawab kaisi tarah chahiye
LIMIT → Kitna chota jawab chahiye

✅ Example:
"Tu ek finance expert hai. SIP ko 5 bullet points mein simple Hindi mein samjhao."
🎙️ "Role, Task, Format aur Limit — yeh 4 parts milake banate hain perfect prompt!"
