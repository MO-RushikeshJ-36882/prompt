## 🥇 Method 1 — MURF AI (Hindi Girl Voice)

### Steps:
```
Step 1 → Go to: https://murf.ai
Step 2 → Click "Try Free"
Step 3 → Select Language = Hindi
Step 4 → Select Voice = Female (Priya / Kavya)
Step 5 → Open script_hindi.txt
Step 6 → Paste each slide script
Step 7 → Click "Generate"
Step 8 → Download MP3
Step 9 → Add to PowerPoint:
          Insert → Audio → Select MP3
Step 10 → Set "Auto Play" on each slide
```
